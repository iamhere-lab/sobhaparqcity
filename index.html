Sobha Limited · Hennur, Bengaluru

SOBHA ParQ City is envisioned as a large-scale, integrated township. The “Q” in ParQ stands for Quest, a journey of discovery: a harmonious balance between modern urban living and nature, offering more than just a home, an enriching lifestyle for every generation.

Sobha is known for quality construction, meticulous finishing and attention to detail. That standard now arrives in Hennur.

SOBHA ParQ City is a master-planned 50-acre land parcel with 3,184 homes across 2 to 4 BHK, from 1,250 to 2,800 sq. ft., with a 100-metre dense forest entrance boulevard, Jacaranda Tree Avenue, exclusive senior living, generous balconies, landscaped courts and water features.
